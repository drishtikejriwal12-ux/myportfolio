I came for commerce and stayed for the people behind the numbers. Business strategy taught me how companies decide. Product thinking showed me who those decisions are really for. The more I learned, the more I cared about the person on the other side, and that curiosity pulled me into UX.

Stop 02 · Interned

Crewnox Innovation Labs

Crewnox Innovation Labs Private Limited, a design studio

UX Design Intern · May – Jul

My first time inside a real design studio, and I loved it. I got a front-row seat to the creative side of business, and to the beautifully messy part nobody shows you: last-minute edits, rushed iterations and everything changing right before the deadline. It taught me that good design is never a straight line.
